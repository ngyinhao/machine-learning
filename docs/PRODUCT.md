# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The student developing the reinforcement-learning assignment and anyone observing the project demonstration. A local browser is the working assumption.

## Product Purpose

Train a warehouse navigation policy and see how its movement, delivery success and reward change through training.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One robot delivers one parcel using four directions and a wait action.
- The simulator records transition events so reward weights can be varied independently of movement rules.
- Training charts and route replays use recorded simulator results.
- The first increment uses a fixed floor plan and synthetic traffic and energy costs.
