# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers and readers reviewing the warehouse navigation experiments in a browser.

## Product Purpose

Visualize how reward and learner choices affect delivery success, travel time, and forklift exposure. Learning curves and route replays connect measured outcomes to individual navigation decisions. The setup controls let readers reproduce runs for inspection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One robot delivers one parcel using four directions and a wait action.
- The simulator records transition events so reward weights can be varied independently of movement rules.
- Training charts and route replays use recorded simulator results.
- The first increment uses a fixed floor plan and synthetic traffic and energy costs.
